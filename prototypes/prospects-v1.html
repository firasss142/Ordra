Prospects — prototype v1

The leads rebuild. Two surfaces: the agent's Prospects tab (new agent language) and the manager's console (existing light console). Mock data: one Libyan market, five agents.

What to check · agent
  1. File tab shows the live banner: hot inbound prospects interrupt the order queue.
  2. Buckets: à appeler maintenant (hot inbound, due callbacks, fresh win-back) → rappels prévus → liste d'appels (campaign, with today's cap) → done.
  3. Row = who, where from, what they want, the customer's history, the next move.
  4. Nouveau prospect: phone first, live match against customers and open prospects, "Enregistrer et commander" for callers who order on the spot.
  5. Résultat d'appel two steps; "veut commander" opens the conversion form prefilled from the customer record. Converted → the queue's "Convertis" bucket, never "Confirmé".
  6. Campaign prospects carry the offer and the script in the drawer.
What to check · manager
  1. KPIs answer "does this produce revenue": delivered revenue from prospects, not just conversions.
  2. Pipeline table, no kanban. Filters by source, agent, status, campaign.
  3. Campaign builder: audience from your own customers, preview count, offer + script FR/AR, distribution rule, daily cap. Results per campaign down to delivered revenue.
  4. Sources: leads → first contact time → converted → delivered revenue, per channel.
