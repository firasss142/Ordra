Darb Sabil Bench

Click-through prototype of the Libyan warehouse agent shell, phone first, 390 × 844. Real product names, the nine Darb Sabil roll colours as published, Arabic copy. Visual language borrowed from the Orders console: white cards on a cool ground, tinted icon holders, big figure with a small bold label, thumbnail + name + product rows, outlined status pills, age turning amber.

Try:

  1. Tap a roll in the rail to work one colour at a time.
  2. Tap خذ الطرد, then ربط الملصق. Change the outcome with the dashed "simulate" control at the bottom of the sheet.
  3. Returns: type 7700888 (found), 1213123 (still at Darb Sabil), 999 (unknown), or press the ⚠ to see the list failing honestly.
  4. Stock: tap a row to expand it.

Design rules: colour is always paired with its name and the branch code on a white plate; semantic states use icon and text, never a filled colour; the sticker field is LTR with a numeric keypad.
