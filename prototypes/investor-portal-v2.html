Ordra · investor gate · devil's-advocate review + prototype

The portal computes, then argues with itself.

Every number below is pulled from production. The current investor home shows four figures about the same money with no arithmetic between them, hides the one question every investor asks first (when is the next payout, and roughly how much), and — more seriously — the engine underneath under-pays by ~5× because it reverses revenue on parcels that were never delivered.

Data: OMS production (Biovera · TN) Prototype clock: 18 April 2026 — the only window where every state (settled, held, withdrawn, live estimate) is populated by real trading Position: 20 000 of 50 000 DT · 40 % (statement basis) Scope: investor screens only; admin gate untouched

Verdict

Three findings, in the order they matter

Business logicrollup.ts · foldLedger

The money is wrong before it reaches any screen.

Two verified defects: returned parcels reverse revenue that was never booked (March net profit reported 1 681,500 — real 8 029,500), and late-return corrections never touch the withdrawable balance. Fix these before touching layout, or the redesign is a nicer frame around a wrong number.

KPIsPortfolioClient · CapitalJourney

It answers accounting questions, not investor questions.

No time dimension (everything is since-inception), no next-settlement date, no return on capital, two gauges that are 100 − each other, a "1.61× multiple" on capital that hasn't been returned, and an ops funnel the investor cannot act on — which is also broken on real data.

Structure4 tabs · 5 stacked cards

The bones are right; the flesh is in the wrong places.

"Disponible" as the hero is correct. But the balance strip, the capital journey and the per-product "votre bénéfice" say the same thing three ways, movements are buried under a tab called Relevés, and the product page opens with a funnel instead of a month.

Business logic — verified against production

What the numbers get wrong

These are not opinions about layout. Each was reproduced with SQL against the live database and read back through the code path.

Critical · under-pays ~5×src/lib/investors/rollup.ts · returned loop

Returns reverse revenue that was never earned

The rollup treats every returned transition as "delivered, then sent back" and subtracts the order's revenue and COGS. In this business a return is a refused parcel — it never passed through delivered. Production has 523 returned orders, 0 of which were ever delivered. The manager's P&L (product-profitability.ts) books revenue on delivered only, so the two surfaces disagree by ~5× on the same product-month.

Biovera · March 2026 · 273 delivered · 162 returned · 40 % share statement (settled) recomputed, no reversal revenue 5 562,500 13 540,500 COGS 1 160,000 2 790,000 delivery / returns 1 638,000 / 648,000 1 638,000 / 648,000 packing 435,000 435,000 net profit 1 681,500 8 029,500 investor share 40 % 672,600 3 211,800 → owed 2 539,200 more

Every future settlement is wrong the same way until the reversal is removed (or gated on a prior delivered row for the same order). The prototype uses the recomputed figures.

Critical · reserve is theatreinvestor-balance.ts · foldLedger

Corrections never reach the withdrawable balance

A correction is folded into pending and lifetimeProfit only. The UI never renders balance.pending (it shows the live estimate instead), so a late-return charge lands in a bucket nobody sees and available is untouched. The reserve is released in full regardless.

production ledger, investor ilyes@oms.local accrual +672,600 settlement +672,600 reserve_hold −67,260 withdrawal −300,000 reserve_release +67,260 correction −67,260 "Late returns after 2026-03-31, charged against released reserve" available = 672,600 − 67,260 + 67,260 − 300,000 = 372,600 withdrawn + available = 300,000 + 372,600 = 672,600 ← full pre-correction share "votre part" shown = 605,340 ← the only place the −67,260 exists

Fix: fold correction into available when it carries a statement_id (settled money), into pending only when it doesn't. Then the "charged against released reserve" note becomes true.

Trust erosionportfolio.ts · estimateUnsettled

The live estimate is systematically higher than the statement

The estimate skips allocated market-wide ad spend and ignores an outstanding carried loss; settlement includes both. An investor watches one number climb all month, then receives a smaller one and is told nothing. In April the gap is small (100 DT allocated → 40 DT), but the direction is always the same, and a carried loss can make it large.

Fix: compute the estimate with the same function as the dry-run settlement, over the open period. Label it once, prominently: "estimation — recalculée chaque nuit, figée à la clôture".

Decided, never shippedinvestor-allocation.ts · activeCapitalInPeriod

Capital is still not day-weighted

docs/investor-domain.md D2 decided pro-rata by days active. The function still counts any overlapping position at full value: fund on the 30th, take a full month's share. The prototype states the rule on the product page so it stops being a surprise once implemented.

Data hygieneinvestor_daily_product_stats · 0 rows

The rollup table is empty in production today

The live portal shows zeros not because nothing traded but because nobody re-ran the backfill. A 3-day trailing window with manual ?date= backfill is not a pipeline. Also: confirmed_count = 0 for a month with 273 deliveries — the funnel is unshowable to an outsider until order_history carries confirmations.

Definition driftportfolio.ts vs product-profitability.ts

"Return rate" means two things

Portal: returned ÷ (delivered + returned). Manager P&L: returned ÷ dispatched. Same word, different denominators, different numbers. The prototype keeps the portal's (closed parcels only — it is the one that maps to money) and names the denominator on screen: "165 retours sur 662 colis clôturés".

Type hierarchy, per screen

Exactly one 28 px figure (design-system §4.15). Then 22 px for the open-period estimate — grey, because it is not money yet. Then 15 px tile values, 14 px money in lists, 12 px context, 11 px uppercase labels. Today's home has a 28, a 20 and three 18s competing.

Colour, per §4.15

Green on money that arrived (réglé, +movements), red on money that left (retraits, costs), grey on money that is not yet money (estimates, retenue). Status hues on the return-rate chip only. Brand green on the CTA and the active tab. Nothing else is coloured.

Delete. Its three tiles become one equation line under the hero and one date on the period card. "Retiré" is history and belongs in Historique.

Decisions

What I would decide now, with defaults

None of these need a workshop. Each has an obvious default; the prototype already assumes it.

1
Cadence: calendar month, statement issued by the 5th, reserve 90 days. Without a stated cadence "prochain relevé" cannot exist and the close depends on an admin remembering. Put it in settings, print it on Compte and on the period card.
2
Remove the return-side revenue reversal in the rollup — or gate it on a prior delivered row for the same order. Then re-issue March as a correction line (+2 539,200), which is exactly what the ledger was designed to do. Add a test: returned without delivered must not move revenue or COGS.
3
Fold correction into available when it references a settled statement. Otherwise the reserve protects nobody. Add a test that a negative correction after reserve_release reduces available.
4
Estimate = dry-run settlement over the open period. One function, two callers. Includes allocated ad spend and carried loss.
5
House position mandatory. Opening an investor position on a product with no house row is refused (or auto-creates one at 0 with a warning). "100 % du produit" for 1 000 DT must be impossible to render.
6
Return rate = returned ÷ (delivered + returned), named on screen. Align the manager P&L or label its own differently. One word, one definition, in one product.
7
Rollup runs nightly over the trailing 30 days, idempotently. The 3-day window with manual backfill is why production shows zeros. Late carrier events are the norm in COD, not the exception.
8
Ship day-weighted capital (D2) before the next close. It was decided; the tests still encode the old rule. State it on the product page.

Order of work: 2 → 3 → 4 first (they change money), then the screens above, then 1/5/6/7/8 as they touch settings and admin. TDD per CLAUDE.md for every step in the first group.
